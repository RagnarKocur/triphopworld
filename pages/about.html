About Trip Hop World

A quiet room in a loud city — curated by Sebastian.

[Portrait / Abstract B&W]

Manifesto

Trip Hop World exists to make visitors fall in love with trip-hop — not just as music, but as atmosphere. Sound, text, and visuals invite you to dance with happy melancholy.

Bio

Curated by Sebastian — photographer, interior designer, and learner of code. This is a living archive of artists, albums, playlists, and quiet poems.

“Trip-hop is the poetry of the urban night.”
