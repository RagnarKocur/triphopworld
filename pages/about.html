The Project

Trip-Hop World is a personal, curated space where jazz textures meet hip-hop rhythms. I aim to make visitors fall in love with the genre — to “dance with happy melancholy”.

This site is built in black & white aesthetics: documentary text, poetry fragments, archival links, and hand-picked playlists. Designed & coded with care in Dublin.

“Trip-hop is the poetry of the urban night.”
