TRICKY

About Tricky

Tricky (born Adrian Nicholas Matthews Thaws) is a British rapper, producer, and actor from Bristol, England. He was a founding member of Massive Attack before embarking on a solo career that would establish him as one of the most innovative and influential figures in trip-hop and alternative music.

His debut solo album "Maxinquaye" (1995) is considered a masterpiece of the trip-hop genre, featuring his distinctive whispered vocals, dark atmospheres, and innovative production techniques. The album showcased his unique approach to blending hip-hop, electronic music, and alternative rock.

Tricky's music is characterized by its experimental nature, political themes, and genre-defying approach. He has collaborated with numerous artists and continues to push musical boundaries, maintaining his status as one of the most original voices in contemporary music.
