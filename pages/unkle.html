UNKLE

About UNKLE

UNKLE is a British musical project founded by James Lavelle in 1992. Originally a collaboration between Lavelle and Tim Goldsworthy, the project has evolved over the years with various collaborators, including DJ Shadow, who was instrumental in creating their groundbreaking debut album.

Their debut album "Psyence Fiction" (1998) was a landmark release in the trip-hop genre, featuring collaborations with artists like Thom Yorke, Richard Ashcroft, and Mike D. The album showcased UNKLE's innovative approach to blending electronic music, hip-hop, and alternative rock.

UNKLE's music is characterized by its cinematic quality, experimental production techniques, and high-profile collaborations. The project has continued to evolve, exploring various musical styles while maintaining its distinctive atmospheric sound and visual aesthetic.
